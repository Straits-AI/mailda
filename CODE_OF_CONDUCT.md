# Code of conduct

Be direct, be specific, be kind. Argue about the code, the measurement and the decision, never about the
person. A claim needs a receipt; so does a complaint.

Unacceptable: harassment, personal attacks, publishing someone's private information, and sustained
disruption of a thread after being asked to stop.

Report a problem to the maintainers through the private channel in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md); the same
address serves both. Reports are read by a maintainer who was not part of the incident where one exists,
and the reporter hears back within a week. Consequences range from a warning to removal from the project,
at the maintainers' judgement, and are recorded in the thread they concern unless privacy says otherwise.
